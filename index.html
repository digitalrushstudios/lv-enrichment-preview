Enrichment Holistic Skin Health is a boutique spa in Las Vegas, NV. Guests are welcomed into a calming space with holistic skin health treatments rooted in intentional care.

When your skin is balanced, you look healthy, bright, clear, and radiant. At Enrichment, each facial is customized to your skin's specific needs.

Most treatments include aromatherapy, skin analysis, gommage, pressure point treatment, a custom mask, and light effleurage of the arms and hands.
