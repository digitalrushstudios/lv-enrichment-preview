Before You Book

Booking & Cancellation Policy

We respect your time — and ask that you respect ours. Here's everything you need to know before your appointment.

📅

How to Book

Book online anytime through Square Appointments. Once confirmed, you'll receive a confirmation email and text with your appointment details and a link to reschedule or cancel if needed.

🔔

Cancellations & Changes

We understand life doesn't always go to plan. Cancellations or service changes must be received at least 24 hours prior to your appointment time. Changes can be made via the link in your confirmation email or text.

⚠️

Late Cancellations & No-Shows

Cancellations within 24 hours and all "No Show / No Call" appointments will be charged a $50 cancellation fee per our No Show Protection Policy. Day-of cancellations will also void any voucher, gift certificate, or service credit associated with the booking.

🎟️

Vouchers & Gift Certificates

If you have a Groupon, LivingSocial, or gift certificate, add your voucher number in the appointment notes when booking. Please note that same-day cancellations or no-shows will void your voucher.

By booking your appointment, you agree to our cancellation policy and terms & conditions. While we always try to accommodate your preferred esthetician, assignments may be adjusted based on availability.
